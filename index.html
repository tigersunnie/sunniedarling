👋 welcome to the studio

sunnie darling

art by Sunnie ☀️

Every morning, before anything else, I make something by hand. It's how I stay connected to what lasts.

I work across acrylic, watercolor, ink, oil pastel, and collage, and I build my feminist collages from vintage found imagery, taking old pictures apart and reassembling them into something new. Beauty, to me, isn't decoration. It's essential.
